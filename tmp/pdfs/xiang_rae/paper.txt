                                                                                         6. Conclusion

                                                                                            To reduce reverberation and improve the performance of active
                                                                                         sonars in detecting moving targets, a method, which formulates
                                                                                         the problem of reverberation suppression and moving target
                                                                                         detection as the estimation of low-rank and sparse matrices, is pro-
                                                                                         posed. The RAE is applied to recover low-rank and sparse compo-
                                                                                         nents of the data corresponding to steady components and target
                                                                                         echo components. The CCA method further highlights the trajec-
                                                                                         tory of the moving target based on the continuity of the target
                                                                                         locations over the acoustic pings. Both the simulation and field
                                                                                         experiment data results demonstrate the effectiveness of the pro-
                                                                                         posed method. Compared with the RPCA method, the SC of the pro-
                                                                                         posed method increased by a factor of 1.09, and the NESR was
                                                                                         reduced by 51.28% on average based on numerical simulation. In
                                                                                         our simulation and experiments, the signal of each ping was one-
                                                                                         dimensional data, which can be extended to a two-dimensional
                                                                                         scale in the future. The proposed method exhibits potential for tar-
                                                                                         get enhancement in various scenarios such as hazard-causing
